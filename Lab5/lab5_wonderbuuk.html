About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

UMT is a public university under Ministry of Higher Education located at Kuala Nerus, Terengganu. This university is especialized in fisheries and marine science studies

This university is strongly committed to Blue Economy framework, driving academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and sustainable innovation that focused on marine and coastal ecosystems.
